DEMAND LETTER
Write a demand letter

A written request to be paid. Many people try one before court. Sending one doesn't commit you to suing.

California expects you to ask before you file:
CCP 116.320(b)(3)-(4); SC-100 item 4

LIVE · [BRACKETS] STILL NEED AN ANSWER

[Your name]

[Your mailing address]

November 9, 2026

[Their name]

[Their mailing address]

RE: Demand for return of security deposit — [what it's about]

Dear [Their name]:

I am writing about my security deposit for [what it's about]. I am asking you to return [amount] of my deposit.

Please pay [amount] by November 23, 2026.

I would prefer to settle this without going to court. If you disagree with any part of this, please contact me before that date so we can try to work it out.

If I do not receive payment or hear from you by then, I may file a claim in small claims court to recover the amount owed, plus any court costs allowed by law.

Sincerely,

[Your name]

Prepared with Fair Claim's free letter tool (general information, not legal advice). Review before sending.
