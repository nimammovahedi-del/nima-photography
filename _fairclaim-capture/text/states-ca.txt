IN SHORT

You speak for yourself: lawyers can't represent either side at the hearing. Most counties offer a free small claims advisor. Hearings are set 20 to 70 days after you file.

Filing the claim

Use form SC-100. File it with the clerk in person, by mail, or online where your court allows it. There are no formal pleadings and no pre-trial discovery.
