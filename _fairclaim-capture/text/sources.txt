GETTING PAID

What studies found about getting paid.

Courts don't track it, so there's no national number. These studies are old and local: a rough picture, not a forecast.

55%

of winners in a study of Denver's small claims court never collected any part of their judgment. Overall, only 31% of the money awarded was ever paid.

Best et al., 21 Fordham Urb. L.J. 343 (1994)

56% vs. 79%

In a 2004 survey of 600 Michigan small claims plaintiffs, 56% of cases decided by a judge got full or partial payment, compared with 79% of mediated cases.

Donohue & Atkin (2004), via Resolution Systems Institute

≈ 2×

In Maine small claims courts, defendants fully complied with mediated agreements at almost twice the rate of judgments imposed by a judge.

McEwen & Maiman, 18 Law & Society Review (1984)

≈ 2 in 3

of small claims winners in three Maine counties didn't collect, according to a 2004 Sun Journal survey; local clerks estimated winners were paid a quarter to a third of the time.

Sun Journal (Lewiston, Me.), Aug. 22, 2004

100,000+

small claims cases were filed in Philadelphia from 2013 to 2018, most of them debt collection cases against individuals. Of the cases that reached a judgment, 95% were defaults.

Pew Charitable Trusts (2022)
