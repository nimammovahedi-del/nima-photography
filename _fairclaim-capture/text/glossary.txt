Abstract of judgment

A short court document that summarizes a judgment. Recording it with a county office creates a lien on real estate the debtor owns in that county.

In California
SEE ALSO

Appeal

Asking a higher court to change a decision. In small claims, who may appeal, how long they have, and whether the appeal is a brand-new trial differ a lot from state to state, and some states allow no appeal at all.

In California
SEE ALSO
Trial de novo

Assignee

ALSO CALLED DEBT BUYER

A person or company that has taken over someone else's right to be paid. Debt buyers are assignees: they buy old debts and then try to collect them, sometimes by suing.
